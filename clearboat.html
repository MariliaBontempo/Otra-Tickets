The Experience

Discover Curaçao's underwater world — without getting wet.

This is the main Clearboat experience and the one most guests book. Departing from Daaibooi Beach, the 90-minute cruise follows Curaçao's west coast past a string of well-known beaches — Porto Mari (often spotted with sea pigs), Playa Hunku and Cas Abou. The transparent floor gives you a view straight down as coral formations and tropical fish drift beneath you, like floating over a well-stocked saltwater aquarium.

There's a stop at the ever-popular Cas Abou, where you can snorkel or swim — or stay aboard with a drink and a light snack while the crew talk through local coral-reclamation work. The captain and first mate are fun and engaging, speak four languages, and share the marine life, coastline and bits of island history along the way.

It's a favourite of the Otra Guide team: an immersive way into Curaçao's marine world for anyone who'd rather not dive or snorkel, and for those who prefer to stay dry and still see what's beneath the turquoise.
